The playbook

Two modes, two different lenses.

Grinders · breakeven-plus-one

Use the game’s live breakeven odds, 1 in N.

buy = floor(N) + 1

Example from the winning session: Fire & Ice Tripler at 1 in 3.35 → buy 4 ($40).

  1. Re-check the breakeven rank the day you buy.
  2. Filter to games actually stocked in the case.
  3. Break ties by biggest live top prize, then most top prizes left, then cheaper ticket.

Long shots · expected top value

Use one ticket from winnings only. Ignore breakeven as the ranking lens.

rank by expected top-prize value per ticket (top $ × top left ÷ tickets left), tie-break by raw top-prize odds

Never fire at a game whose top tier is gone. The $20 leader is the default while its $500 tier remains stocked.

Why the grinder rule works for the goal

In today’s top 20, breakeven-plus-one puts the chance of at least one breakeven-or-better ticket around 71–81%. It does not change the game’s negative expected value; it targets more winning sessions per dollar.

Independent tickets can still produce a net loss even when one ticket breaks even.

Per-game payout % is paywalled behind ScratchOdds Gold, so rankings use live breakeven + top-tier data from the free tier. Prize counts move as tickets sell; re-check live data before buying. Entertainment, not investment advice; no strategy guarantees a profit.
